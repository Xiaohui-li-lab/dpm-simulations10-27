\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Divination is not mere superstition but a cognitive-symbolic technology for reading the present quantum state of an intended object. This paper proposes that, when an inquirer fixes attention on a target, the resulting mental intent constitutes a specific ``mind-intention quantum state.'' This state perturbs the surrounding spacetime through field coupling, causing a randomized divinatory instrument to resonate with it and to output an oracle symbol that is phase-matched to the target. Drawing on ten divination records collected between 23 and 26 October 2026---covering self-divination, socio-political divination, and planetary-scale divination---I derive the Principle of Same-Phase Attraction and Opposite-Phase Repulsion (SPAO): quantum states of similar structure or phase attract and cohere, whereas dissimilar states repel and segregate. The principle is applied to magnetic field lines, social aggregation, political belief, and the diplomatic strategy of ``freezing the present.'' Three testable predictions are offered, together with a reproducible Divination Quantum-State Protocol (DQSP).
\end{abstract}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} quantum state; divination; same-phase attraction; opposite-phase repulsion; mental intent; Guanyin oracle lots; I Ching; MOS principle; magnetic field lines; international relations; Mahakaruna Dharani

\section{Introduction}

The ancients read the cracks of tortoise shells and the fall of yarrow stalks not to manufacture prophecy, but to disclose the configuration of a given moment \cite{Li2026,IChing}. The present manuscript reinterprets this practice: divination is the empirical registration of a quantum state. Every event, individual, or collectivity begins as a quantum state; the oracular symbol is a coarse-grained projection of that state. Once the present quantum state is known, its future evolution---following the Manifestation-Origination Sequence (MOS)---becomes intelligible \cite{Li2026}.

The essence of divination is this: the inquirer silently concentrates on a question, and the mind-intention quantum state thereby becomes the quantum state of the matter inquired into, exerting direct influence on the surrounding environment and constraining the quantum environment of the divinatory instrument, so that a corresponding oracle text appears \cite{Li2026}. Divination is therefore not a guess about the future, but a precise diagnosis of the present; the future is simply the further unfoldment of that present quantum state according to the MOS sequence.

\section{Theoretical Framework}

\subsection{Divination as Quantum-State Probing}

The core operation of divination is intention fixation. When the inquirer focuses on a target, consciousness generates a mind-intention quantum state. This state is not closed; it extends into surrounding spacetime, creating a quantum field centered on the inquirer. If a divinatory instrument (oracle lots, hexagrams, random book pages) is then deployed under conditions of maximal stochastic freedom, it couples to the dominant mode of that field and outputs a symbol phase-matched to the target. The oracle text is thus not an arbitrary linguistic game but a coarse-grained projection of the target's quantum state.

\subsection{The MOS Principle}

The Manifestation-Origination Sequence (MOS) denotes the process by which microscopic quantum configurations differentiate, amplify, and precipitate into macroscopic events \cite{Li2026}. A divination result therefore reveals not a mechanically deterministic future, but the developmental phase of the target state---incipience, growth, climax, or decline. This perspective bridges the present and the future: new quantum states continually participate, grow, and eventually become observable events.

\subsection{The SPAO Principle}

From repeated correspondence between mental state and oracular output, I infer the Principle of Same-Phase Attraction and Opposite-Phase Repulsion (SPAO):

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Same-phase attraction:} quantum states of similar structure or phase attract, cohere, and manifest together;
  \item \textbf{Opposite-phase repulsion:} quantum states of dissimilar structure or phase repel and segregate.
\end{itemize}

This principle operates at the level of quantum-state topology and stands in layered contrast---possibly a helical inversion---with the macroscopic magnetic rule that opposite poles attract and like poles repel.

\section{Materials and Methods}

\subsection{Divination Quantum-State Protocol (DQSP)}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Intention fixation.} The inquirer silently concentrates on the target.
  \item \textbf{Field induction.} The mind-intention quantum state perturbs the local quantum environment.
  \item \textbf{Randomized coupling.} The divinatory instrument is operated under maximal stochastic freedom (Guanyin oracle lots, random page opening).
  \item \textbf{Interpretive mapping.} The resulting symbol is semantically matched to the target situation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Dataset Construction}

Data were collected from 23 to 26 October 2026, comprising a three-day self-divination sequence followed by remote divinations on multiple collective targets. Each record includes date, target, mental intent, divination result (lot number and text), and interpretive conclusion.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Magnetic Field Lines}

SPAO offers a new micro-level explanation for the formation of magnetic field lines: iron filings align along field lines because the particles at those locations occupy same-phase quantum states and attract one another; the blank regions between lines correspond to opposite-phase repulsion. The macroscopic rule that like magnetic poles repel and opposite poles attract may then be understood as a helical inversion of the underlying SPAO order \cite{Feynman1964}.

\subsection{Social Aggregation and Human Relationships}

Empirical rules such as ``birds of a feather flock together'' and ``one takes on the color of one's company'' receive a unified quantum-state interpretation: interpersonal affinity reflects phase matching of mind-intention states. Friendship cliques, harmonious families, fellowship of the virtuous, evil thoughts, and righteous thoughts can all be regarded as distinct quantum states. The lovers' intuition that ``hearts are linked by a single thread'' is likewise a manifestation of same-phase attraction \cite{Li2026}.

\subsection{Political Belief and Spiritual Cultivation}

Political belief functions as an individual's supreme mind-intention quantum state. If this state remains unsubdued, long-term Buddhist practice may yield little effect; conversely, when political belief becomes phase-matched with cultivation goals, practice enters an accelerated state. The Great Compassion Mantra can be regarded as a quantum state of supreme wisdom; individuals or civilizations phase-matched with it receive natural protection, while those phase-mismatched may encounter repulsion and disaster \cite{Dharani}.

\subsection{``Freezing the Present'' in International Relations}

In international relations, ``freezing the present''---halting conflict at its current boundary and deferring final resolution to the future---can be understood as an intentional quantum operation in the domain of consciousness: adversaries create a shared quantum state by collectively accepting the idea of freezing the present, thereby converting opposite-phase repulsion into same-phase attraction. The SPAO principle suggests that durable peace is more likely to emerge through same-phase attractive mechanisms such as trade and reciprocity than through military deterrence \cite{Li2026}.

\section{Testable Predictions and Benchmarks}

To move the framework from conceptual study to testable science, I propose the following predictions and benchmarks:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Reproducibility benchmark.} Independent inquirers following DQSP in a double-blind design with $N \geq 100$ trials should obtain oracle symbols whose semantic correlation with the preset intention exceeds chance levels ($p < 0.05$).
  \item \textbf{Magnetic phase mapping.} Under high-resolution scanning, iron filings aggregated along magnetic field lines should exhibit coherent spin-domain orientations, whereas the blank regions between lines should show a statistically distinct orientation distribution.
  \item \textbf{Social-network prediction.} After controlling for demographic variables, pairs of individuals who self-report high congruence in political-spiritual mindset should score significantly higher on relational intimacy measures than random dyads.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

Once divination is reconceived as a quantum-state diagnostic technology, it becomes a bridge between the world of consciousness and the macroscopic world. The SPAO principle unifies oracle correspondence, magnetic lineation, social aggregation, political belief, and diplomatic strategy within a single quantum-state framework. If the above predictions are independently verified, it would imply that human intention is an active variable in the construction of reality rather than a passive spectator.
